# 01 - Website Expansion And Information Architecture
Status: Approved
Owner: Adam
Created: 2026-03-15

---

## Purpose

Move the website beyond a single landing page into a focused Estate Planning marketing site that can:

- explain the product clearly
- support conversion at different buyer stages
- hold richer proof and product context
- support content and outbound campaigns

---

## Strategic Position

LawOps should be presented as:

- the Estate Planning operating system
- the workflow control and firm intelligence layer
- a serious operating platform for ownership, consistency, and visibility

The site should not expand into broad law-firm-for-everyone positioning yet.

---

## Recommended Site Map

### 1. Homepage

Purpose:

- establish the category
- explain the problem and why LawOps is different
- show operational proof
- drive fit conversations

### 2. Estate Planning Page

Purpose:

- make the wedge explicit
- explain the Estate Planning workflow model
- show retained-rate, critical-date metrics, stale matter visibility, and scorecards
- speak directly to Estate Planning owners and operators

Working angle:

- "The operating system for Estate Planning firms"

### 3. Platform Page

Purpose:

- explain the core operating model in product language
- practice area -> stage -> critical date -> task
- dashboards, whiteboards, scorecards, workflow controls
- position LawOps as the control layer, not generic SaaS

### 4. Intelligence Page

Purpose:

- go deeper on firm intelligence
- show the owner-facing value
- explain stale matters, on-time work, pipeline progress, retained rate, critical-date timing, and team reliability

### 5. Why LawOps / Why Not Generic Software Page

Purpose:

- sharpen differentiation
- explain boxed software vs operational control
- handle objections around "why not just use our current stack"

### 6. Contact / Fit Conversation Page

Purpose:

- create a more intentional conversion destination than footer mailto only
- qualify buyers
- frame budget/fit expectation

### 7. Insights / Blog

Purpose:

- support founder authority
- support SEO and outbound collateral
- create reusable content for social and sales follow-up

---

## Execution Order

### Phase A

- refine homepage
- add contact / fit page
- add Estate Planning page

### Phase B

- add platform page
- add intelligence page
- connect stronger CTA paths between pages

### Phase C

- launch insights / blog structure
- add foundational articles
- support outbound and SEO

---

## Design Direction

The expanded site should:

- stay premium and structured
- use real product visuals and diagrams over stock photography
- keep a consistent visual system across pages
- make screenshots do explanatory work, not decorative work

---

## Content Rules

Each page should have one clear job.

Homepage:

- category and conversion

Estate Planning:

- wedge specificity and relevance

Platform:

- system explanation

Intelligence:

- proof and owner value

Why LawOps:

- differentiation and objection handling

Contact:

- qualification and conversion

Insights:

- authority and demand support

---

## Guardrails

- do not overclaim AI-native execution before product truth supports it
- do not broaden beyond Estate Planning prematurely
- do not let additional pages become thin filler
- do not use stock-lawyer imagery as the core visual strategy

---

## Definition Of Done

- [ ] homepage refined after initial rewrite
- [ ] contact / fit conversation page exists
- [ ] Estate Planning page exists
- [ ] platform page exists
- [ ] intelligence page exists
- [ ] clear navigation across pages exists
- [ ] CTA system is intentional across the site
- [ ] page copy stays aligned to app-side product truth
