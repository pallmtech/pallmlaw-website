- educate Estate Planning firms on operational maturity
- expose hidden workflow drag
- build founder authority
- create assets that feed website, social, outbound, and sales calls

Primary themes:

- workflow chaos hides in handoffs, not headlines
- stale matters are an operational tax
- critical dates should run the work, not sit in a spreadsheet
- accountability beats heroics

Preferred content formats:

- founder opinion posts
- teardown posts on broken law firm workflows
- practical framework articles
- short case-style narratives based on recurring operational patterns
- comparison pieces against generic SaaS categories

Repurposing rule:

- one blog post should yield multiple social posts, one email angle, and one sales-call talking point
