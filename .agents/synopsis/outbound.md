# Outbound Synopsis

Primary outbound goal:

- generate qualified Estate Planning fit conversations

Target roles:

- owner / founder attorney
- managing attorney
- firm administrator
- operations lead

Best opening angles:

- stale matters and invisible bottlenecks
- lack of accountability across handoffs
- no operational visibility across open matters and tasks
- no retained-rate or cycle-time intelligence

Offer style:

- invite a fit conversation
- offer a workflow diagnostic
- avoid forcing a demo on first touch

Cold email rules:

- short
- pain-specific
- no hype
- one clear CTA
- personalized to role and practice type
