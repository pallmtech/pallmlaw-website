# Estate Planning Launch Packet

Status: Draft
Owner: Marketing Director

## Objective

Establish LawOps as the operating system for Estate Planning firms that need tighter workflow control, clearer accountability, and better operational visibility.

## Recommended market entry thesis

Estate Planning firms rarely fail because they lack software.
They fail because work lives across memory, spreadsheets, disconnected tools, and inconsistent handoffs.

LawOps should enter by naming that operational pain directly.

## Homepage angle

Lead with:

- workflow control
- visibility
- accountability
- firm intelligence

Do not lead with:

- generic automation
- private AI
- broad law-firm-for-everyone claims

## Core proof points available now

- workflow model structured by practice area, stage, critical date, and task
- open matters and open tasks visibility
- average completion / pipeline progress visibility
- percent of tasks not overdue
- stale matter detection
- Estate Planning retained-rate reporting
- Estate Planning timing metrics across delivery stages
- team performance scorecards by person

## Suggested CTA

- Book a Fit Conversation
- Request an Estate Planning Workflow Diagnostic

## Content priorities

1. Why Estate Planning firms lose margin in the handoff layer

3. Why critical dates should drive work
4. Why law firms need operational intelligence, not more dashboards
5. Why AI does not fix a broken workflow
