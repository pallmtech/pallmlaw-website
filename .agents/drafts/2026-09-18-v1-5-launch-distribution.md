## Founder LinkedIn Post

Most workflow failures in a law firm do not look dramatic. A deadline sits outside the operating view. An automated action is expected, but nobody can see what is coming next. A matter waits between owners without creating enough noise to attract attention.

PallmLaw v1.5 focuses on that operating layer.

The release makes critical dates first-class operational records, adds matter-level automation visibility, gives administrators a firm-level Automation Monitor, connects relevant client communication history to the matter, and improves the reliability of the daily dashboard.

Estate Planning remains our primary focus. The underlying workflow foundation also supports configured Probate, Probate Release, and Trust Administration processes.

This is not an AI-first release. It is a workflow-control release: clearer ownership, more visible automation, and better operational context for the people responsible for moving matters forward.
