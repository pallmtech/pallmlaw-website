# CMO Priority Escalation: Pre-Retained Conversion Workflow

Date: 2026-03-15
Source Repo: `LawOps - Website`
Role: Marketing CMO
Audience: Product Director

## Purpose

Escalate pre-retained / retained-rate / conversion workflow from interesting feature idea to serious product-priority discussion.

Marketing assessment:

- this may be the strongest near-term commercial differentiator available to LawOps
- this may deserve elevation into a major next-phase product priority rather than remaining a side concept

## Why This Is Being Escalated

Current marketing view is that LawOps is credible today as:

- workflow control
- operational visibility
- Estate Planning bottleneck intelligence

However, those strengths can still be perceived as internal process improvement.

Pre-retained conversion workflow changes the commercial story.

If LawOps can help an Estate Planning firm manage:

- consults
- pending retainment
- follow-up discipline
- retained-rate visibility
- next-best action before engagement

then LawOps moves closer to a system the owner pays for because it affects revenue, not just internal hygiene.

## Strategic Judgment From Marketing

If emerging differentiators are ranked by commercial potential, current marketing ranking is:

1. pre-retained / retained-rate / conversion workflow
2. Estate Planning operational visibility and bottleneck intelligence
3. AI-assisted communication and drafting inside the workflow
4. generic customization language

Marketing judgment:

- the first item may be the bridge between "useful internal system" and "system the owner will pay serious money for"

## Why This Matters Specifically In Estate Planning

Estate Planning appears especially suited to this direction because:

- consult-to-retained is a meaningful conversion point
- firms likely leave revenue on the table in follow-up
- the workflow is structured enough to make pre-retained tracking operationally useful
- this creates a cleaner line from intake -> retained -> delivery

This may give LawOps a stronger and more owner-relevant wedge than workflow control alone.

## What Marketing Is Not Asking For

This is not a request to become a generic CRM or broad sales platform.

Marketing recommendation would be a narrow first version focused on:

- consult volume
- retained volume
- pending pre-retained matters
- follow-up tasks and next actions
- retained-rate reporting
- aging / delay visibility between consult and retained

## Product Questions Requiring Director Response

1. Does product agree this should be evaluated as a major next-phase priority rather than a side feature?
2. Does the current model already contain enough retained-status or pre-engagement primitives to support a narrow first version?
3. Would this require a net-new planning phase, or can it be incorporated into already emerging roadmap work?
4. What is the narrowest credible version that could materially test the commercial thesis?
5. If product disagrees, what does product view as the stronger next differentiator instead?

## Request

Please respond through a future product handoff with:

- product judgment on whether this should be elevated in priority
- roadmap posture: near-term / later / out-of-scope
- whether app-side approved plans should be updated to reflect this as a major candidate
