## Launch Posture

- LawOps v1.2.0 is in beta / release-candidate posture.
- Estate Planning is the strongest current wedge.
- Probate should not be claimed as fully live until client workflow configuration is imported, tested, and accepted.
- AI positioning must stay disciplined: read-only, scoped, audited, and user-attributed.

## Strong Claims Supported

- LawOps is the operating system for Estate Planning firms.
- LawOps helps Estate Planning firms handle more matters with the team they already have.
- LawOps helps Estate Planning firms replace manual follow-up, scattered spreadsheets, and invisible bottlenecks with a structured operating system for matters, workflows, client communication, and firm intelligence.
- LawOps provides visibility across matters, tasks, notes, deadlines, stages, team workload, workflow status, reporting, and firm intelligence.
- LawOps supports configurable workflow automation around legal matter operations, including practice area, stage, critical date, task, deadline, matter context, and conditional workflow logic.
- LawOps supports client-facing email templates, firm-branded message copy, workflow-triggered client communication, and communication event traceability.
- LawOps supports task assignment email notifications and task-level execution notes.
- LawOps supports Estate Planning retained-rate reporting and critical-date timing metrics.
- LawOps supports Estate Planning plan type / matter subtype classification across operational surfaces.
- LawOps supports controlled, read-only AI connector access through approved tools.
- LawOps can expose controlled, read-only operational intelligence to approved AI clients through governed MCP tools.
- LawOps AI access is auditable down to external client, LawOps user token activity, tool calls, status, timing, and errors.
- LawOps is built around per-firm data posture and portable instance operations.
- LawOps includes unlimited internal user access as part of platform-access positioning.

## Product Capabilities To Show Visually

- Main dashboard with Notes navigation.
- Matter record surface showing matter, notes, stages/tasks, dates, and context.
- Email template list and editor.
- Client-facing email preview.
- Automation flow canvas and email action configuration.
- Automation run / delivery visibility.
- MCP access and agent activity showing client, user, tool, status, and timing.
- Firm admin / brand controls where relevant to per-firm ownership.

## Claims To Avoid

- Do not say AI agents can update LawOps records.
- Do not say MCP gives AI direct database access.
- Do not say LawOps performs legal analysis, legal advice, or AI legal drafting.
- Do not say AI sends client emails or executes automation.
- Do not market AI as autonomous legal work.
- Do not present LawOps as a full email marketing platform.
- Do not claim Probate workflows are live for all clients.
- Do not imply one user authorizing Claude or ChatGPT gives access as every user.
- Do not use invented testimonials, logos, customer counts, ROI figures, or fake proof.

## Positioning Guidance

- Lead with operating-system language for Estate Planning firms.
- Keep the pitch short: `Track. Automate. Optimize.`
- Use the six-pillar public structure: Visibility Across Every Matter, Automated Workflows, Automated Client Communication, Firm Intelligence, AI Agent Connected, More Coming Soon.
- Frame workflow automation around matter execution, not generic CRM automation: matter lifecycle automation, stage-based task automation, deadline-aware workflows, operational handoffs, wait steps, and run traceability.
- Frame communication automation around client experience consistency, staff follow-up relief, firm-controlled messaging, stage-aware emails, matter-aware reminders, and audit-ready communication history.
- Treat AI as a governed connected layer, not the main category: approved AI clients can ask practical operational questions through scoped MCP tools without direct database access or write permissions.
- Explain capabilities as one system: matter visibility, workflow automation, client communication automation, firm intelligence, and approved read-only MCP tool intelligence.
- Public website copy should never expose internal marketing mechanics such as `SEO page`, `search-focused page`, or `content asset`.
