This branding guide is designed to feel like a high-performance industrial tool rather than a trendy software app. It prioritizes clarity over decoration, reflecting the transition from chaos to calm operations.
________________________________________
1. Brand Identity & Strategy
The North Star
LawOps is a workflow automation system for law firms that replaces spreadsheets, ambiguity, and post-its with clear stages, dates, and visibility.
The Posture
•	Calm: We don't scream; we resolve.
•	Operational: We care about how the work actually gets done.
•	Confident: We provide the "Opinionated OS" so the firm doesn't have to guess.
•	Minimal: Every element serves a functional purpose.

3. Typography
We use Inter for its neutral, highly legible "system" feel. It is the gold standard for user interfaces and operational clarity.
Primary Typeface: Inter
•	Headings (Bold): Used for the "Ops" in LawOps and primary section headers.
•	Body (Regular): Used for all data entry, matter stages, and descriptions.
•	Mono (Optional): Use Inter Tight or a system Mono font for "Dates" or "Matter IDs" to emphasize the data-driven nature of the OS.
The Hierarchy:
•	H1: Inter Bold | Charcoal | 32px | Tight Tracking
•	H2: Inter Semi-Bold | Ops Blue | 24px
•	Body: Inter Regular | Charcoal | 16px | 1.5 Line Height
•	Labels: Inter Medium | Mid-Gray | 12px | All Caps (for Stage Names)

4. Logo Construction: LawOps
The logo is a wordmark, not an icon. It communicates that the system is the foundation.
•	The Text: LawOps
•	The Weight: "Law" is light/regular; "Ops" is bold.
•	The Divider: A 1pt vertical divider in Light Gray or a simple Blue dot can be placed between the two words if extra separation is needed for the wordmark.
•	The Slogan: "Operating System for Law Firms" sits below in Mid-Gray, Inter Regular, small-caps or standard sentence case.
Logo Rule: Never use a gavel, a scale, or a gear. The word "LawOps" is the brand.
________________________________________
5. Visual Language & UI
Since LawOps is "Opinionated," the design must reflect "The Way."
•	Stages: Represented by flat, rectangular blocks. No gradients. No rounded "pill" buttons. Use Sharp corners (2px-4px radius max) to feel professional.
•	Ownership: Names or initials in clear, small circles.
•	Visibility: Use the Action Yellow for "Past Due" or "Action Required" only. If everything is yellow, nothing is important.
•	The Grid: Use a strict 8px grid system. Operations are about precision; the design should feel like it was built on a drafting table.
________________________________________
6. Brand Voice (The "Adult" Filter)
•	Avoid: "Revolutionize your workflow," "Gamify your firm," "Unlock your potential."
•	Use: "Identify the bottleneck," "Visible ownership," "Predictable outcomes."

1. UI/UX Framework: The "Opinionated" Grid
To achieve the brand posture of Confidence and Structure, we use a strict 8px Grid System. Every margin, padding, and height must be a multiple of 8.
Layout Architecture
•	Global Navigation (The Sidebar): Fixed at 240px. Color: Ops Blue (#243746). Icons are White or 80% opacity.
•	The Workspace: Color: White (#FFFFFF) or Light Gray (#E4E4E7) background. This is where the work happens.
•	The Utility Bar (Right Rail): Collapsible. Used for "Critical Dates" and "Active Notifications."
UI Components
•	Buttons: * Primary: Action Yellow (#F2BC1B) with Charcoal (#1A1A1A) text. No rounded pills. 4px corner radius maximum.
o	Secondary: Ops Blue outline with White background.
•	Matter Stage Cards: * Background: White.
o	Border: 1px Light Gray (#E4E4E7).
o	Status Indicator: A 4px vertical bar on the left of the card. Blue for "Active," Yellow for "Action Needed."
•	Data Inputs: Simple, underlined or thin-bordered boxes. No heavy shadows.

2. Dashboard Concept: "The Command Center"
The dashboard is not a chart of "Total Revenue." It is a visibility tool for "Current Matters."
Section A: The Pipeline (Top)
A horizontal progress bar showing the 5-6 non-negotiable stages of an estate planning matter (e.g., Intake → Design → Drafting → Review → Signing).
•	Visual: Stages that are complete turn Ops Blue. The current stage has a subtle Action Yellow underline.
Section B: The "Stalled Matters" View (Middle Left)
A list of matters where the "Critical Date" has passed or no ownership has been assigned.
•	UX Note: This uses the Action Yellow accent. It draws the eye immediately to where the "Chaos" is creeping back in.
Section C: The Visibility List (Middle Right)
A feed of "Who is doing what right now."
•	Format: Matter Name | Stage | Owner (Initials) | Days in Stage.
