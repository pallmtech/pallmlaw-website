# LawOps: The Operating System for Modern Law Firms

## The Vision
**LawOps** is not just a CRM; it is the **Operating System** for the modern legal practice. Built to replace fragmented toolsets, LawOps provides the core infrastructure that powers matter management, workflow automation, and firm intelligence in a single, unified platform.

In an industry where "process is product," LawOps transforms the way law firms operate from the ground up—moving from reactive task management to proactive, data-driven execution.

---

## ⚡ Core Capabilities

### 1. Dynamic Matter Lifecycles
Define custom stages for every practice area—from Estate Planning to Probate. Visualize your entire portfolio as it moves through the pipeline, with clear visibility into bottlenecks and progress.

### 2. The Smart-Workflow Engine
LawOps doesn't just store data; it acts on it. 
- **Auto-Triggering Tasks**: Tasks populate automatically based on stage entry, date changes, or prerequisite completions.
- **Cascade Operations**: Deep logic that ensures when a matter moves, its entire history and associated records move with it, instantly.
- **Webhook Intelligence**: Real-time synchronization with external intake tools (like DecisionVault), bridging the gap between client intake and case management.

### 3. Critical Date Infrastructure
Never miss a deadline. LawOps tracks up to 20 custom critical dates per practice area, with auto-calculated milestones and "Populate Date" badges that keep your team focused on what’s next.

### 4. Firm Intelligence & Telemetry
View your firm’s health at a glance.
- **On-Time Performance**: Real-time metrics on how your team is meeting internal deadlines.
- **Portfolio Progress**: Visual dashboards that track volume, efficiency, and growth.
- **Operational Benchmarks**: In-depth telemetry provided by the "Developer Labs" to optimize system performance.

### 5. Developer-First Foundation
LawOps is built for firms that want to scale.
- **Key Vault**: Secure, user-specific API management for high-fidelity audit logs.
- **Platform Infrastructure**: A robust backend (powered by the Twenty CRM core) that handles heavy data volumes without breaking a sweat.
