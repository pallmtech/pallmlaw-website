# LawOps Product Direction Notes

Date Captured: 2026-03-15
Status: Strategic notes from founder / CMO working session

## Current Strategic Product Shape

### Phase 1

Operational control layer

Meaning:

- practice area
- stage
- critical date
- task
- dashboards
- stale matter detection
- team accountability
- workflow visibility

### Phase 2

AI-led execution inside the workflow, human monitored

Includes:

- AI assistance during execution
- AI-generated work product / drafting / communications
- human verification as a required control

### Phase 3

Intake plus security maturity and safe storage

Includes:

- stronger intake experience
- secure storage path
- deeper integration posture
- more formal trust posture such as SOC 2 readiness/maturity

---

## Near-Term AI Opportunity

The founder believes AI assistance may be viable now in email template messaging.

Near-term practical use cases:

- draft email copy variants inside the communications template area
- rewrite a message into clearer client language
- convert internal workflow state into client-safe status updates
- suggest tone variants while preserving firm review

Guardrail:

- do not message this publicly as full AI communication automation without product confirmation

---

## Matter-Level Client Communications Concept

Potential feature:

- send an email to the client directly from the matter page with a status update

Potential product framing:

- client communications component
- matter-specific update snapshot
- visual workflow roadmap showing where the matter is in the process
- example narrative: "this is where your estate plan is on the road to peace"

Potential value:

- lowers client uncertainty
- improves perceived responsiveness
- makes workflow visible externally, not just internally
- creates a more human, trust-building Estate Planning experience

Guardrail:

- current product handoff does not yet support strong public claims here
- treat this as a strategic concept unless and until product confirms public readiness

---

## Pre-Retained / Sales Opportunity

Potential major differentiator:

- LawOps could support pre-retained follow-up and conversion workflow

Founder conviction:

- law firms are likely leaving money on the table before retainment
- the ability to manage sales-side follow-up inside the same operating system could be a real differentiator

Strategic significance:

- this could separate LawOps from tools that only begin after the matter is active
- it creates a bridge between intake, conversion, and legal workflow
- it may strengthen the Estate Planning wedge significantly

Marketing implication:

- if validated in product, this could become a major positioning angle
- however, it should not be claimed publicly until product truth confirms actual capability

---

## Document / Work Product Direction

Founder view:

- to be truly differentiated, LawOps likely needs a path into work product, not just workflow oversight

Practical interpretation:

- start with supervised drafting support
- use narrow document families first
- combine templates, framework extraction, and human review
- avoid pretending the system replaces legal judgment

---

## Cost And Feasibility Notes

Founder concerns:

- LLM cost
- limited budget
- difficulty of training a specialized model

Working strategic answer:

- do not train a custom model first
- use constrained AI assistance around specific workflows
- start narrow by document family, matter type, and jurisdiction
- use human review gates

---

## Liability Notes

Founder concern:

- LawOps must not be treated as a lawyer or legal decision maker

Product implication:

- software-only posture
- human review required
- final responsibility remains with the firm
- output should be positioned as assistance/draft support, not authoritative legal advice

Legal implication:

- future terms, product UX, and review controls will matter if work product features expand

---

## Infrastructure And Security Notes

Founder concern:

- whether current sovereign architecture can mature into a serious law-firm trust posture

Working conclusion:

- prototype pragmatically
- long-term trust posture likely requires stronger infrastructure discipline
- SOC 2 becomes more relevant as LawOps touches communications, work product, and sensitive storage

---

## Strategic Summary

LawOps is strongest when it is not sold as:

- another law firm software suite
- a task app
- generic AI automation

LawOps becomes more defensible if it evolves into:

- workflow control
- supervised AI execution
- work product support
- communication infrastructure
- intake-to-delivery operational spine
